About TalentMatch

A learning prototype for job discovery and explainable candidate matching.

How jobs are aggregated

Sample listings from three mock sources are normalized into one feed. Duplicates are identified using normalized job title and company names. Live scraping and external job APIs are not connected.

Transparent score formula

Every score is calculated from four weighted components.

Required skills60%
Nice-to-have skills20%
Experience fit10%
Location / remote fit10%
Score =60% × required coverage
+ 20% × nice-to-have coverage
+ 10% experience fit
+ 10% location / remote fit
Important limitations
  • Sample job listings and candidate profiles only.
  • Matching is a simplified, deterministic rule-based model.
  • Score is a learning aid, not a prediction of hiring success.
  • Do not use this demo score to screen real candidates.
  • Data is stored locally in this browser using localStorage.
